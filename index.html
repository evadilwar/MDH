Who we are

A trusted, independent bridge between community need and organisational capability.

Compass Wellbeing CIC was created to bring people and organisations together to make a real difference in people’s lives. We are a not-for-profit Community Interest Company, owned by the ‘outstanding’-rated East London NHS Foundation Trust – the first NHS ‘Marmot Trust’.

  • We understand local communities
  • We understand the health and care system
  • We understand the VCFSE sector
  • We understand how to work with corporate organisations in ways that create real value for communities
“

We are not here to replace other organisations. We are here to connect, convene, support and, where needed, deliver.

Our ambition is to be a trusted and independent partner that helps communities thrive and helps systems work better together.

How we create impact

Four connected priorities.

Shaped by what we heard from communities, partners, ELFT, stakeholders, our Board and our staff.

1

Strengthening partnerships and system integration

People’s lives do not fit neatly into organisational boundaries. We bring together communities, VCFSE organisations, NHS partners, local authorities and businesses – building trust, improving collaboration, reducing duplication and creating shared solutions.

“Compass Wellbeing is a trusted partner that understands both communities and systems” – Partners

2

Improving equity, access and prevention

Too many people only receive support when problems have already become serious. Helping people earlier creates better outcomes – directly supporting ELFT’s priorities around prevention, earlier help, improving access and advancing equity.

13,000+ people reached this year – 91% through prevention or early intervention

“It was easier to get support” – Communities

3

Strengthening communities through social value and opportunity

Social value is the way we create impact: employment and employability, volunteering, skills, wellbeing programmes, community investment, community leadership and capacity building.

£4.4m directed into 85 community projects in 2024/25 · 445 people supported towards work against a target of 400

“There are more opportunities available to me” – Communities

4

Building a stronger, more sustainable Compass Wellbeing

Financial sustainability enables our purpose. We will diversify income, strengthen business development, scale what works and create new partnerships – a sustainable social enterprise model that reinvests back into communities.

£24m+ directed to the VCFSE sector since 2021 · 475+ contracts awarded · Living Wage Champion 2026

“Compass Wellbeing has a clear and credible model for sustainable growth” – Partners
